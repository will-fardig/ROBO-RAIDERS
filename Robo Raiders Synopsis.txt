Robo Raiders

Plot Synopsis: It is 2040; you were selected as a new astronaut for the Mars missions. Everything was going well for your team, and you maintained contact with Ground Control. Then you experienced interference as you entered Mars's atmosphere, crashing your ship.

You woke up with nothing but a small pistol and hordes of robots and alien masters from the Andromeda Galaxy charging at you. They have launched an invasion of the Milky Way, with you being caught in their crosshairs. Survive the onslaught, salvage their tech, and find a way to return home!
